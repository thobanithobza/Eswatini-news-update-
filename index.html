Weather in Manzini Today: Warm With Afternoon Showers

20 November 2026 | By Thobani | Manzini

Expect a warm day in Manzini today with temperatures reaching around 28 degrees. There is a chance of afternoon showers, so remember to carry an umbrella if you are going out.

In Mbabane it will be slightly cooler, while in the Lowveld it will be very hot. Drink a lot of water and stay in the shade during midday.

Farmers are happy with the rain, saying it is good for their crops this season.
